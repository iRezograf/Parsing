Festivals 2026

You can’t beat a good festival. Singing at the top of your lungs, dancing all night long, sitting on your mate's shoulders as that one banger kicks in - it’s pure poetry.

If, like us, you’re planning on filling your year with sun, tunes, pints, and laughs, you’re in the right place. There are loads of festivals in 2026 to suit any music fan, from hedonistic electronic havens to indie dreamlands and everything in between.

Join us in discovering the best UK festivals right on your doorstep or in glorious, sun-kissed destinations. See you there!
